Electrical Engineering Intern @ General Dynamics Mission Systems

Supported the Hammerhead Encapsulated Effector project as an EE intern working with the Systems I&T team. Analyzed and designed new product drawings for cable and circuit boards. Designed a test cable enabling faster and more efficient FAT (Factory Acceptance Testing), saving the company from needing to use a $1.25+ million device per test evaluation. Completed NPRs (New Product Requests) while importing new PCB designs into the companies Enterprise PDM/Windchill ecosystem. Improved and redesigned project state machine and basic tech documentation for easier customer readability. Regularly tested and diagnosed cables and boards for shortage and continuity issues. Worked with C, Rust, LTSpice, & Altium.

Summer 2026 Position Brief

The General Dynamics Mission Systems Hammerhead is a modern, moored anti-submarine mine system developed for the U.S. Navy. It acts as an encapsulated, torpedo-armed weapon designed to anchor to the sea floor in deep and intermediate waters to detect, track, and destroy enemy submarines. As an EE intern supporting the Systems I&T team, it was my job to support Hammerhead using my electrical and computer engineering knowledge. As the only dedicated EE, I took on a diverse number of project types that broadened my view of the electrical side of system's life cycle.
