NREIP Intern

Naval Research Enterprise Internship Program (NREIP) is a competitive 10‑week summer research internship hosted by the U.S. Department of the Navy that places students in Department of Navy laboratories to support real research and engineering projects under direct mentorship. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

During my time as an NREIP intern at Naval Undersea Warfare Center (NUWC) Division Newport, RI, I worked as an embedded systems engineer focusing on:

  • Embedded software development in C and C++
  • FPGA board support and hardware‑software interfacing
  • Sensor technology integration and testing
  • Python scripting for data analysis and automated tests

Key Responsibilities

My work involved taking ownership of small core engineering tasks and integrating them into larger systems. Responsibilities included writing and debugging firmware, validating hardware functionality, and collaborating with senior engineers to bring designs from concept to prototype.

Technical Highlights

I gained experience with hardware‑oriented development workflows including version control (Git), build systems, continuous integration, and test automation. I also learned how to read detailed hardware datasheets and debug complex embedded systems in real time.

Professional Growth

The internship emphasized hands‑on research participation, mentorship, and exposure to real engineering problems. This experience strengthened my technical skills and clarified my future engineering career goals.
