NREIP Intern

Naval Research Enterprise Internship Program (NREIP) is a competitive 10‑week summer research internship hosted by the U.S. Department of the Navy that places students in Department of Navy laboratories to support real research and engineering projects under direct mentorship.

I've completed two sequential years in the Naval Research Enterprise Internship Program at NUWC Division Newport. While at NUWC I received two years' experience as an embedded systems engineer. For Summer 2025, I worked within a hands-on, high workload environment in which I was presented countless embedded systems and electronics projects to complete. This experience forced me to think critically about my knowledge from class and the stream of new information I was receiving every day on the job. I primarily worked with C, C++, and Python to develop software for FPGA boards, sensor technologies, and more. For Summer 2024, I joined the computer architecture team in which I grew more comfortable developing projects individually and with team members. These projects mainly consisted of developing Python and C software meant to support the larger goal of the team. I also participated within the test bays alongside multiple Integration and Testing teams in order to get an in depth of view of the NUWC ecosystem. I'm proud to say that my contributions, while small in scale, were necessary for my team's progression and will be used long after my internship has ended.
